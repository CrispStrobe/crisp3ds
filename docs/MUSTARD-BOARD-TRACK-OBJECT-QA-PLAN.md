## Neutral board-frame distribution audit

Use the unchanged triangulated points in checker-square units; do **not** align to a reference object or fit a bottle primitive. Report finite-point count; x/y/signed-height min, p5, median, p95, max; robust 5–95% axis spans; covariance eigenvalues and ratios; a coarse 3D occupancy histogram; and per-track nearest-neighbor distances. List track IDs at distribution extremes and the number of observations per track. These are descriptive diagnostics, not a learned bounding box or grounds to retune the existing height/reprojection/parallax thresholds. Nonfinite coordinates, mismatched counts, or source-hash drift fail QA; surprising but finite shape/distribution is flagged for image review, not silently discarded. Save at most a small JSON summary plus a deterministic ID-to-coordinate audit table under a fresh external directory; no COLMAP model.

## Bottle-versus-board/support review

Create bounded two-scale crops centered on **every one** of the 1,089 raw observations from original TRAIN RGB, grouped by stable track ID and labeled with exact filename and pixel location. Include a full-frame locator thumbnail so a crop on a bottle edge is not mistaken for adjacent support. Render from original RGB only; checkerboard corners, masks, and pose-derived projection may annotate locations but may not determine the class. The coarse support masks are corroboration only, not object-only proof. A reviewer assigns each observation `bottle surface`, `board/support/background`, or `uncertain`; tracks also receive a whole-track label. A track is `bottle-only` only if **all** its observations are visibly bottle surface in their source frames and none is uncertain or on board/support/background. Any conflicting or insufficient image evidence excludes that whole track; no partial deletion of observations to improve a fit. Record per-class track/observation counts and the excluded IDs/reasons. This is deliberately conservative: a feature visible in only one convincing view does not pass just because its triangulation reprojection is low.

For a proposed fixed-pose sparse handoff, require at least **eight** bottle-only tracks (the existing contract minimum), at least **12 distinct posed TRAIN filenames** in their union, and at least one pair of those views whose image-derived camera-center azimuths about the board origin differ by **90° or more** (wrapped smaller angular difference). These are coverage floors, not shape fitting. If camera azimuth is ill-defined or these floors fail, report `unavailable`; do not relax them after inspection. Do not claim object-only status from a random subset or from a montage too small to judge location. A second reviewer should resolve uncertain labels only from the same sealed TRAIN RGB; unresolved cases stay excluded. Preserve a per-observation decision table and reviewer/procedure provenance in a new bounded receipt, with no feature descriptor, virtual-image, sparse-model, or dense export.

The next implementation review should first settle crop sizes, output byte cap, reviewer workflow, exact azimuth computation, synthetic tests for all-observation track labeling and coverage, and disk/time/RSS limits. Only after the QA receipt is sealed and separately reviewed should a distinct proposal consider virtual undistorted images and the fixed-pose sparse contract. No evaluation against scanner geometry or supplied camera poses may determine which tracks survive.
